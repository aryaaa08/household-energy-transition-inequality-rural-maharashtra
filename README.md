# Household Energy Transition and Inequality in Rural Maharashtra

### Rural Immersion Fieldwork | Tata Institute of Social Sciences (TISS)

A field-based study of household energy consumption, expenditure, fuel stacking, and energy transition in rural Sangamner, Maharashtra.

## 📍 Project Overview

This project was conducted as part of the **Rural Immersion Fieldwork** at the Tata Institute of Social Sciences (TISS). Primary household-level data were collected through a field survey conducted across villages in **Sangamner, Maharashtra**.

The survey questionnaire was inspired by the broad structure of the **NSSO Household Consumption Expenditure Survey** and covered multiple dimensions of household consumption, expenditure, living conditions, and resource use.

While the collected dataset covers several sections of the questionnaire, this project focuses specifically on the variables relevant to **household energy use and transition**.

## 🔎 Research Focus

The study examines:

- Household fuel consumption and energy expenditure
- Use of traditional and modern energy sources
- **Fuel stacking** — continued use of firewood alongside LPG
- Differences in energy use across villages
- Environmental impacts of household energy consumption
- Carbon emissions and pressure on forest resources
- Potential pathways for a cleaner and more affordable energy transition

## 📊 Analysis

The project combines field observations with quantitative analysis, including:

- Village-level energy categorisation
- Household consumption and expenditure analysis
- Spearman's rank correlation analysis
- Carbon-emission estimation
- Environmental impact assessment
- Policy scenario modelling
